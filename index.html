05 / Interpretation & actions

Where to focus next

01 — Activate more invitees

The overall learning cohort has 29,123 invitees who did not join. Review invitation delivery, sign-up friction, course relevance, and reminders. Track invitation-to-join conversion by batch.

02 — Protect the January renewal wave

The source lists 50,577 expirations in January 2027. Segment learners by recent activity, target re-engagement messaging, and confirm renewal options before the first January deadline.

03 — Build a targeted Gen AI pilot

Investigate a practical Generative AI pathway linked to Business, Data Science, and IT. Pilot demand with learner surveys and completion metrics rather than assuming the current chart proves Gen AI demand.

04 — Resolve data definitions

Confirm the Overall Learning slide’s 65% and 49% labels versus its counts, the meaning of “active” versus “engaged,” and whether batch-level expiration counts overlap. The revised slides do not report learning hours.
